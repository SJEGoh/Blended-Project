Adversarial vs LSM-trained hedge, under attack

Seller of a Bermudan put (50 exercise dates, Merton jumps λ = 1) hedges to minimise CVaR0.9 of the loss. The adversarial (GDA) hedger is trained against a stopper that learns to exercise at the worst time for the seller. The LSM baseline hedger is trained assuming the holder follows the risk-neutral Longstaff–Schwartz exercise rule. Afterwards each frozen hedger is attacked by a fresh adversarial stopper and by simple exercise rules. Lower CVaR is better. Watch it on a live path →
